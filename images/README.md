# Images

- `memorial/`      your own photographs of the National Military Memorial (Part A / field photos)
- `archive-room/`  sample or contributed items for the archive room (only with the contributor's consent)

Filenames used by the site are listed in `js/data.js` (ABSENCE_IMG and GALLERY).
Tips: use .jpg, keep each file under ~500 KB (resize before uploading), lowercase names, no spaces.
Do not commit anyone's private family photos, letters or recordings without their consent.
